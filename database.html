Database ERD

Draft Entity Relationship Diagram for the G.U.A.R.D.I.A.N. secure edge-AI driver monitoring and fleet management system.

Current Draft — V0.1: This ERD is designed around the actual G.U.A.R.D.I.A.N. operating model. Passenger, moderator, route manager and admin are the four login roles; drivers and conductors are operational entities and do not receive login accounts. This is a conceptual draft, not the final normalized schema. [Weekly Progress II]

Core Design

Drivers are monitored entities rather than login accounts. DRIVER_TRIP acts as the central record connecting driver, bus, route, telemetry, live monitoring, scores and alerts.

Planned Refinement

Cardinalities, route assignment history, notification/escalation timing, agency scoping, scoring thresholds, retention rules and final constraints will be refined as development progresses.
